# 01 · Brand Foundation

## Esensi

> **MAKE EXPERTISE SCALABLE.**
> Repskill turns the knowledge of your best people into skills your whole sales team can build.

- **Kategori:** AI-powered Sales Capability Platform
- **Alur nilai:** Expertise → Capability → Performance
- **Purpose:** Make great sales expertise scalable.
- **Vision:** A world where great sales expertise can be learned, practiced, and scaled.
- **Mission:** Help organizations capture their best sales knowledge and turn it into continuous learning, practice, and coaching.

## Alur produk (Product Philosophy)

Selalu ditampilkan dengan urutan dan penamaan yang sama:

| #   | Stage    | Fitur / Studio    | Pesan singkat                             |
| --- | -------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 01  | CAPTURE  | Extraction Studio | Extract company & top-performer knowledge |
| 02  | LEARN    | Learning Path     | Turn knowledge into structured learning   |
| 03  | PRACTICE | Scenario Studio   | Simulate realistic sales situations       |
| 04  | REFLECT  | Reflection Studio | Understand what happened and why          |
| 05  | IMPROVE  | (seluruh tim)     | Build capability across the team          |

Fondasi bersama: **Knowledge Universe**, yaitu pengetahuan yang sudah di-review, dikurasi, dan di-approve.
Penulisan baku: `Capture → Learn → Practice → Reflect → Improve` (pakai panah `→`).
Secara visual, stage 01–04 memakai Amber Gold (dot/garis), sedangkan stage 05 (Improve) memakai **oranye** sebagai hasil akhir.

## Personality

| Sifat           | Artinya di website                                              |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SMART           | Klaim spesifik dan kredibel. Tidak hype.                        |
| PRACTICAL       | Contoh situasi sales nyata (pricing objection, discovery call). |
| HUMAN           | Teknologi yang mendukung, bukan robotik. Hindari jargon AI.     |
| CONFIDENT       | Kalimat aktif dan tegas.                                        |
| ENCOURAGING     | Membangun tanpa menghakimi ("gap", bukan "failure").            |
| ACTION-ORIENTED | Setiap section mengarah ke langkah berikutnya (CTA/link).       |

**Archetype: Mentor × Builder.** Mentor: knowledgeable, supportive, guiding. Builder: structured, scalable, performance-oriented.

## Voice & tone (copywriting)

- **Clear:** satu ide kuat per baris/section. Mudah di-scan.
- **Confident:** "Practice a scenario", bukan "You can practice".
- **Approachable:** coach, don't lecture.
- Judul memakai Title Case dan boleh diakhiri titik ("From Expertise to Capability.").
- Eyebrow: UPPERCASE, 1–4 kata ("HOW REPSKILL WORKS").
- CTA utama: **Book Demo** (tombol di form: "Request My Demo"; di Pricing: "Talk to Sales"). CTA sekunder: "Explore …", "See …", "View …".
- Jangan menulis angka/hasil customer yang belum terverifikasi. Pakai placeholder yang jelas ("Placeholder — publish with
  verified results only.").

Contoh baris brand: _Capture expertise. Build skills. Scale capability._
